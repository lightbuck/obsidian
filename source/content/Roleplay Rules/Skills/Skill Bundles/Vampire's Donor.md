*You often allow a Vampire to drink your blood. This, whether inadvertently or not, created a powerful link between you and the Vampire.*
**Cost:** 3
**Skills Gained:**
Replace 'Vampire' with the name of the Vampire that feed on you in these skills
- Attune (Vampire Status Sense) +3
	Sense the physical and mental state of Vampire when they are nearby, how far away you can sense them is determined by your skill tier.
- Manifest (Polarized Blood) +3
	Drop some of your blood in a liquid, the blood will float toward Vampire's Current Location no matter the distance, but how precisely and how quickly depends on your skill tier.
