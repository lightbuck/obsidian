A bond is the connection between two Player Characters, it signifies a dynamic that the characters can have and it leads to advantages in combat and with skill checks.
## Bond Types and Sides
Each Bond has two sides, and these two sides can be of different types. For example character A thinks of character B as a rival, but character B sees character A as a close friend. Each bond type dictates how characters can aid each other during skill checks, and they should inform how to flavor [[Traits#^0609c3|Crossover Actions]]
### Types of Bonds
- Rival: "Let me show you how it's done!" A sees B as a Rival, so whenever B fails a skill check, A may try the same skill check with a +2
- Ally: "Let me help you with that!" A sees B as an Ally, so whenever B attempts a skill check, A may increase the result with one of their own Generic Skill Bonuses.
- Idol/Teacher: "My master never fails!" A sees B as their Idol/Teacher, so whenever B attempts a skill check, A also rolls using B's Skill Bonus. B may then choose either result.
- Admirer/Student: "Pay attention!" A sees B as their Admirer/Student, so whenever B fails a skill check, B may reroll using A's Skill Bonus.
