Stars are the aims, values and themes of the campaign. GMs and Players normally choose together what the Stars of a campaign are, choosing a North Star and an Algol Star and any other number of Stars.
# The North Star
The North Star is the term used for the most important Star of the campaign, the one that should be at the core of what you are doing. The North Star should be a single sentence, framed as something the GM says the Party will do. Here are some examples of some good North Stars:
- You will defend those who can't defend themselves.
- You will discover the unknown.
- You will bring happiness.
- You will bring order.
- You will get rich.
- You will defend nature.
## Following the North Star
When a Player Character acts in accordance with the North Star the GM may grant you a bonus, usually this will come in the form of a few [[Boons|Boon Points]], but the reward could be an extra learned action if the deed performed was of great importance. The GM should refrain from rewarding extra Armaments for the players.
# Algol Stars
Algol Stars indicate something the Player Characters will want to avoid doing, but might struggle to do. Whether or not the point of a campaign is to eventually violate an Algol Star is up to the GM. 
An Algol Star should be a single sentence, framed as something the party says they won't do. Here are some examples of some good Algol Stars:
- We will not kill.
- We will not turn a blind eye to suffering.
- We will not harm nature.
- We will not reveal our identities.
- We will not take sides.
# Other Stars
Any other Star that is not a North Star or an Algol Star does not have mechanic implications. However other Stars are still good to establish and discuss to manage campaign expectations.
