## Role
Foes have Roles, which determine their function in combat through the actions they have access to
### Hitter
These Foes move toward characters and strike them in melee range
### Shooter
These Foes move away from characters and strike them from afar
### Snipers
These Foes strike characters at range, they cannot move but they deal high damage.
### Protector
These Foes remain close to a chosen Foe and protect them #PH
### Controllers
These Foes can issue orders to other Foes to make them act on the Controllers turn or they can aid their allies. #PH
## Ranks
Foe Ranks determine their APs pool, their [[Health and Stamina]] and what kind of Roles they have access to
### 1. Grunts

^e3bd6a

Rank and file combatants, they don't have [[Health and Stamina|Stamina]] and they usually have 1 [[Health and Stamina|Health]], these foes pose a threat to the party by their numbers. They usually work in groups and target a single character. They usually have 1 AP each
Roles:
- Hitter
- Shooter
- Protector 
### 2. Knight
These Foes will challenge Characters in single combat, they usually have [[Health and Stamina|Stamina]] and their effective Health should be enough to survive one round while fighting a single character. They usually have 3 AP
- Hitter
- Shooter 
- Controller 
### 3. Roaches
These Foes have high [[Health and Stamina|Stamina]] but low [[Health and Stamina|Health]]. They usually have 2 AP
- Hitter
- Shooter
- Controller
### 4. Heavies

^4cc935

These Foes always target the Highest Rank Foe or the closest enemy Character, they don't have [[Health and Stamina|Stamina]] but they have a lot of [[Health and Stamina|Health]]. They usually have 2 AP
- Hitter
- Protector 
- Controller 
- Sniper
### 5. Captains

^dbb12a

These Foes have a large amount of [[Health and Stamina|Stamina]] and [[Health and Stamina|Health]]. They usually have 4 AP. They also have access to two Roles and act twice per round
- Hitter
- Shooter 
- Controller 
- Sniper
