# Banes V2

^fa936f

## Physical
You may pick only one of these.
- Fragile (+2)
	-1 Health
- Slow (+2)
	-3 Speed
- Weak (+2)
	Each turn you have an Empowered Gain Tax of 3
## Mental
Skill checks are penalized when one of these may apply. You may pick only one of these. You may discuss with the GM a custom Mental Feature for your character.
- Fear of Dying (+1)
- Homesick (+1)
- Rageful (+1)
- Selfish (+1)
- Close Minded (+1)
- Paranoid (+1)
- Uncaring (+1)
...
Your custom mental negative feature should come up often enough to be a regular problem, but it shouldn't shut down all of the character's roleplay
Example of something too specific: Fear of spiders
