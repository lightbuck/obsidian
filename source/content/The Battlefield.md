# Walls And Cover
## Walls

^6e6320

A wall is something that exists within the enviroment that stops both movement and line of sight
## Partial Cover
A piece of cover is something that exists within the enviroment that stops line of sight for characters that aren't adjacent to it, but can be moved through.
## Full Cover

^4eb84d

A piece of cover is something that exists within the enviroment that stops line of sight for all characters but can be moved through.
