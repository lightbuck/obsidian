# V2
## Feats of Power ^acac8c
Feats of power grant player characters reliable ways to gain empowered. Every PC starts with three slots that can be filled with these feats. No Feat can be chosen more than once.
### From Power
Feats 'From Power' are passive abilities that grant [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]]
[[Powers]]will grant you a free From Power Feat, but you may also choose from this list
- From Success
	Gain 1d6 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] when you exhaust a foe's stamina or when you knock them out
- From Hurt
	Gain 2d6 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] when your stamina is exhausted
- From Swiftness
	Gain 2d4 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] every time you move your full speed
- From Action
	At the end of your turn Gain 3d4 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] if you used 4 different [[Actions#^8c5a75|Core Actions]] or [[Actions#^22357c|Combo Actions]] this turn
- From Dedication
	Gain 3d4 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] when you spend 3 Action points or more in a single [[Actions#^8c5a75|Core Actions]] or [[Actions#^22357c|Combo Actions]] during your turn
- From Overkill
	Any time you deal damage equal or greater to twice the stamina pool of your target you gain 2d8 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]]
- From Impact
	Gain 1d6 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] when you cause a foe to become [[Impacted Condition|Impacted]]
- From Rise
	Gain 2d6 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] when you remove the [[Impacted Condition]]
- From Teamwork
	At the end of your turn gain 1d6 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] for every AP spent on actions with the [[Aid Trait]]
- From Overflow
	Any time you would gain stamina above your [[Health and Stamina#^059ed3|Maximum Stamina]] you instead gain an equal amount of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] ^a04714
### To Power

^40632d

Feats 'To Power' are active abilities that convert stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] into useful buffs. Take a [[Actions#^ffdf5f|Flourish Action]] to activate a To Power Feat
- To Devastation
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]], the next time you roll damage increase the size of all your damage dice by a step for every 10 stacks consumed
- To Brutality
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]], the next time you roll damage add +1d6 damage to the result for every 5 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]]
- To Strength
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]], any time you roll damage this turn increase the result by 2 for every 5 stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]]
- To Resilience
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] gain one stack of [[Conditions#^9f2db4|Protected]] for every 5 stacks
- To Speed
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]], for every 2 stacks consumed you receive a stack of [[Energized Condition|Energized]] 
- To Adrenaline
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]], for every 7 stacks you receive a stack of [[Adrenaline Condition|Adrenaline]] 
- To Stamina
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] the next time you roll your recovery die, increasing the result by one for every 2 stacks
- To Consumption
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]]. Your two next actions this turn gain a [[Spend Trait]] with a value equal to half the stacks of  [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] consumed
- To Allies
	Consume all stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]]. Split your stacks of [[Empowered Condition|Empowered]] equally among any amount allied characters.
