This section will teach you how to play SFF, these steps will explain each part of the system as it becomes relevant during character creation. Read each chapter in full before trying to follow what it says.

## 1) Finding a Character Concept
The first step to creating a character is knowing who you want to make, you may already have some ideas such as "I want to play a character who shoots fire!" or "I want to play a martial artist!". Your first step is to read the descriptions  (the italic text!) of [[Powers]] to find one that fits what you wish to play, or one that gives you inspiration on what to play. Each Power has its own lore and a small snippet on how a character using said power would wield it in combat! 
After finding something interesting, you have two choices: you could either choose to make a character very good at using the chosen Power, or you could find a second Power to add with the first one!

If you do choose to choose to wield two powers, you need to prepare yourself to learn the ins and outs of the system and learn them well. Because while using two powers can absolutely lead you to make a very strong and interesting character, it will be more complex and you will have to manage a greater mental load during play. If that doesn't sound fun for you, you may want to use only one power!

Choosing to wield a single power means you will likely want to look at [[Armaments]] for inspiration on how exactly your character fights. Armaments and their Aspects are an abstracted way to look at what your character uses to fight their Foes with, they are written so that they allow for a large degree of freedom. Here are some examples of combinations of Armaments and Aspects:
- An [[Alkean Magic|Alkean]] Bone Spear: a [[Melee Armament]]
- The burning palms of [[The Phoenix Talon]]: an [[Area Armament]]
- A sniper with curving bullets: a [[Ranged Armament]] with the [[Reaching Aspect]]
- A hired swordsman who fights for you: a [[Melee Armament]] with the [[Companion Aspect]] and the [[Independent Aspect]]
- A mechanical exo-suit that shoots beams of plasma: a [[Ranged Armament]] with the [[Defensive Aspect]]
- An orbital laser: an [[Area Armament]] with the [[Remote Aspect]]

Something should have became obvious by the end there, some of these Armaments could fully define a character regardless of what power you chose. This is also an option in SFF ! This is the secret third way to make a character! The expectation is that you would pick between [[Active Exceed]], [[Trajectory Divination]] or [[Flare Herald]] as your power, each of these corresponds to Melee, Ranged or Area Armaments as flavor neutral options for you to spotlight your armament rather than your Power.
