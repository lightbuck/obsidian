This section will teach you how to play SFF, these steps will explain each part of the system as it becomes relevant during character creation. Read each chapter in full before trying to follow what it says.

## 1) Finding a Character Concept
The first step to creating a character is knowing who you want to make, you may already have some ideas such as "I want to play a character who shoots fire!" or "I want to play a martial artist!". Your first step is to read the descriptions  (the italic text!) of [[Powers]] to find one that fits what you wish to play, or one that gives you inspiration on what to play. Each Power has its own lore and a small snippet on how a character using said power would wield it in combat!

After choosing your character's Power you may want to look at [[Armaments]] and their Aspects. Armaments are your character's weaponry, they come in three types: [[Melee Armament|Melee Armaments]], [[Area Armament|Area Armaments]] and [[Ranged Armament|Ranged Armaments]], granting access to the corresponding type of Attacks. A simple armament can be anything from a sword to some thing specific, like [[The Phoenix Talon|The Phoenix Talon's]] jets of fire emanated from your character's palms.

You may choose to add [[Aspects]] to your Armaments, Changing your Armaments' abilities and functionalities. If you can think of a weapon you can likely find a combination of Armaments and Aspects that reflects how that weapon works. For example a simple sword would be a [[Melee Armament]] but adding a [[Reaching Aspect]] would allow you to turn it into a rope dart. Alternatively you could instead add the [[Defensive Aspect]] to make it into a sword and shield. Or you may even opt to add another Armament as an Aspect, such as the [[Ranged Armament]] to turn it into a gunblade.

Some combinations could be special enough that they could fully define a character regardless of what power you choose. This is also an option in SFF ! The expectation is that if you do that you would pick between [[Active Exceed]], [[Trajectory Divination]] or [[Flare Herald]] as your power. Each of these supports either Melee, Ranged or Area Armaments respectively, they are flavor neutral options for you to spotlight your Armament rather than your Power.

There is a third option, you may like the idea to play with two powers instead of one. This option is significantly more mechanically complex ! I do not recommend it for newer players, but it still an option. The implications are too involved with the mechanics of the system to be described in this first chapter, so it will be discussed further ahead.
