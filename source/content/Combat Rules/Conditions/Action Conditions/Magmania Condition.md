The next time you make a [[Melee Trait|Melee Attack]] increase the damage dealt by the Attack by the number of stacks, then remove this Condition. The next time you take damage you may decrease it by the number of stacks of this Condition and then decrease the stacks of this Condition by one.
You may only ever have up to 6 stacks of this condition.
Any stacks of the [[Protected Condition]] become this Condition instead.
