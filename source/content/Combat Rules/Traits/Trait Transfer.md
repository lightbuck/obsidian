As you increase your level, you may gain access to the ability to add [[Traits]] to [[Actions]]. When you do sure to mark them differently from the starting [[Traits]] of your [[Actions|Action]].
The selection of which [[Traits]] you can add is limited by #PH Traits you already have access to from your [[Powers|Power]].
# Flexing A Trait
In combat you may use a [[Stylish Actions|Stylish Action]], consuming two Stacks of the [[Power]], to temporarily make so that the previous or the next [[Actions|Action]] gains a trait for this turn.
