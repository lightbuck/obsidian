*The Ravenki are able to voluntarily access The First Dream, the dimension where all life yet to be born exists. This allows Ravenki access to an unlimited amount of special kind of Life Force that is yet untethered from physical matter, it is called Somniram. In practical terms, Ravenki are able to rip Somniram out of The First Dream and use it to blast their foes. However this process is likely to backfire and it requires great concentration to be used safely.*
