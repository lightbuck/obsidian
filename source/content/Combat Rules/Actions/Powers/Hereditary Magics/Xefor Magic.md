*The Xefor are born of the magma rivers of their home world Estrar I, whose magic allows them to reshape their bodies in a protracted process colloquially called reforging. Xefor broadly split in two groups, waybound and homebound. The former are the Xefor who travel the galaxy, the latter are the Xefor who remain on their home world. These two groups prefer radically different body types, the skybound Xefor prefer taller and stronger forms, while the homebound Xefor prefer smaller and sturdier forms. That said, regardless of body type Xefor are always resistant to heat thanks to the rocky exterior and the magma that flows in their veins.*
