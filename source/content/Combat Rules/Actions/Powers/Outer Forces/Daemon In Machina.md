*Many fear the Daemons, many see the Daemons and are tempted by their promises. But there are also those who see Daemons as just another Tool to be used. One of these people was a Resono Inventor, who invented the first Daemonic Gunblade. A type of weapon capable of absorbing the Spirit Energy generated by its strikes to then feed it to the Daemon in a controlled way. The name of the inventor of the Daemonic Gunblades is lost to time, but their invention remains and is used by those brave enough to try to capture a Daemon.*
