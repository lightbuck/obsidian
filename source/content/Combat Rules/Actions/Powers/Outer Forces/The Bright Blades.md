*The Bright Blades is the name given to those who know how to wield weapons built around special fragments of sunlight. These fragments are capable of releasing massive quantities of heat, when subjected to physical stress. So much heat in fact that these weapons often melt in the hands of unexperienced Blades. To use these extremely powerful weapons one must be both skilled and ready to suffer.*
