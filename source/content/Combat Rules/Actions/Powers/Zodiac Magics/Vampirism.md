*Vampirism is a Curse, a literal Dark Magic Curse. Those affected are no longer able to gain sustenance from food and become highly sensitive to Sunlight. However in exchange they gain increased strength, sharp nails and the ability to siphon Paracausal energies, magic, through the use of fangs. The siphoned energies not only sustain the Vampire, but cause them to become more powerful and in some rare case, they may even mimic the siphoned magic.*

--- 

Actions:
- **The Frail**, 1 AP
	- *You swipe at your foe, aiming to make them suffer more than anything*
	- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 1d4 damage and they gain one stack of the [[Impacted Condition]].
	- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
- **The Wretched**, 2 AP
	- *You dig your teeth into your Foe, but fail to siphon them leaving behind a bloodied mess*
	- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 2d10 damage. If you this action deals damage to Health, the Foe gains Stacks of the [[Impacted Condition]] equal to the damage dealt.
	- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
- **Joining You, Finally**, 3 AP
	- *You taste your own blood and it sends you in a frenzy, making you swipe at your Foe wildly*
	- Effect: Target one Foe, deal inflict a stack of the [[Impacted Condition]] for every Health you and the Target are missing combined, additionally deal 1d6 damage by that amount. 
	- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Surprising Trait|Surprising]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
- ###### The Fragile, 6 AP
	- *You unleash your hunger and prey upon a Foe, siphoning not just their magic but their identity as well.*
	- Effect: Target a Foe, remove 2 Health from them. If this action knocks out the Target you may remove up to two [[Wounds]] and restore two points of Health. Until you take 2 damage you may take an action that the Target has access to at no AP cost during your turn. 
	- [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]] [[Final Trait|Final]]

Combo Actions: 
- **Complication**, 1 AP
	- *You double down on a Foe, digging your claws and twisting them not to actually wound them, but to provoke as much pain as possible*
	- Condition: Your previous action inflicted the [[Impacted Condition]]
	- Effect: The Target gains another stack of the [[Impacted Condition]], if the Target becomes [[Vulnerable Condition|Vulnerable]] because of this action you gain one stack of the [[Adrenaline Condition]]
	- [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
- **The Big Come Down**, 2 AP
	- *You grab a Foe and dig your fangs into them, finally able to siphon them.*
	- Condition: Your last action inflected the [[Vulnerable Condition]]
	- Effect: Target the Foe you made [[Vulnerable Condition|Vulnerable]], deal damage to health equal to the amount of Action Points you had left before you took this action. Additionally gain stacks of the [[Adrenaline Condition]] equal to that amount. If this action knocks out the Target you may remove a [[Wounds|Wound]] and restore one point of Health.
	- [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Consumed Trait|Consumed]]

	- Effect: The Triggering Foe gains one stack of the [[Impacted Condition]]
