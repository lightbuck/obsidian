*Bloodsten replaces a wielder's limb with a metal that the user can morph into any shape they desire.*

---
Actions:
- **Cymbal**, 1 AP
	- *You shape your Bloodsten prosthetic into a defensive form, aiming to protect yourself, such as a shield or a piece of armor.*
	- Effect: Gain the [[Forging Condition|Forging(Cymbal) Condition]], then, roll your recovery die.
	- [[Charge Trait|Charge]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
- **Snare**, 1 AP
	- *You shape your Bloodsten prosthetic into an agile and more flexible form, such as a whip or a spear.*
	- Effect: Gain the [[Forging Condition|Forging(Snare) Condition]], 
	- [[Charge Trait|Charge]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
- **Tom**, 1 AP
	- *You shape your Bloodsten prosthetic into a blunt and heavy form, such as a hammer or a mace.*
	- Effect: Gain the [[Forging Condition|Forging(Tom) Condition]], 
	- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
- **Drum**, 1 AP
	- *You shape your Bloodsten prosthetic, in a large and cutting form, such as a claymore or an axe.*
	- Effect: Gain the [[Forging Condition|Forging(Drum) Condition]], 
	- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
- **Solo**, 2 AP
	- *You reshape Bloodsten prosthetic returning it to the form of its body part and fixing it.*
	- Effect: Roll your recovery die thrice and pick two results to gain. If you have it lose the [[Forging Condition]] and instead gain all three results.
	- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]

Combo Actions: 
- **Single Stroke Roll** 1 AP
	- *You rapidly attack with your Bloodsten prosthetic, surprising your Foe with your transformation.*
	- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
	- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 1d8 damage.
	- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Surprising Trait|Surprising]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
- **Single Ratamacue**, 2 AP
	- *You deliver a powerful slam with your Bloodsten prosthetic.*
	- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
	- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 2d6 damage.
	- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Brutal Trait|Brutal]]
- **Double Drag Tap**, 2 AP
	- *You reinforce your Bloodsten prosthetic.*
	- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
	- Effect: roll your recovery die twice and choose a result to add to your stamina as normal, while the other result is gained as bonus damage to your next attack.
	- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]

Reactions: 
- **On The Downbeat**, R
	- *You quickly prepare yourself*
	- Condition: Combat begins
	- Effect: Take a main action from [[Bloodsten]]
	- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
- **Polyrhythmical Cross Counter**, R
	- *You are hit by a foe, but you push through the pain and hit back.*
	- Condition: You are targeted by a Foe with an Attack
	- Effect: After resolving the Triggering Attack, deal 1d6 damage to your Foe.
	- [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
