*Some call it a disease, some call it a curse, but talk to any werewolf and they'll tell you a different story. A story of self discovery, of embracing one's untamed side, of giving in to the temptation to howl at the moon and finding true freedom.

Becoming a Werewolf is a process requiring multiple steps. The individual must sleep with the moonlight hitting their face, for seven nights in a row. Many usually opt to sleep directly on the surface of a Moon to ensure to be affected by its light. On the seventh night, the individual will experience a dream in which they experience the death of King Lycan, a real event which preceded transformation of King Lycan into a Werewolf and the first known case of Lycanthropy, hence the name. If the individual does not act to preserve their own life within the dream they then will awaken capable of transforming at will, which they do by recalling the dream itself.

Physically speaking Werewolves are still largely humanoid in appearance, although they take on wolf like traits such as a tail, claws, fur covering their body, a canine head. After their first transformation some werewolves permanently retain some wolf like traits such as canine ears or sharp teeth, which traits if any is up to chance.*
