#WIP 
*All Humans have the ability to exceed the natural limitations of their body. Most fighters do so subconsciously in moments of high stress, but some have learned to use this magic intentionally, allowing them to accomplish awe inspiring feats worthy of legend on a regular basis.*

---
%%Movement Melee, multiple effects poly%%
[[Skills]]:
- "Exceed Knowledge"
- Fleet
- Flex

#### Actions:
##### Siege Engine, 1 AP
- *You charge forward, destroying anything in your path*
- Effect: Move in a line for double your speed. Destroy any cover in your path. If during this movement you become adjacent to a Foe, you may choose to spend an extra AP and end the movement at that point and Target that Foe. The Target takes 1d8 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Poly Trait|Poly]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Move Trait|Move]]
##### Earth Shaker, 1 AP
- *You slam a Foe into the ground*
- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 1d6 damage. If the Target already had the [[Impacted Curse]] or would gain it because of this action, you deal an extra 1d6 damage. You may spend an extra AP to add the [[Fling Trait]] with a value of 10 to this action.
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Poly Trait|Poly]]
##### Burst Dash, 1 AP
- *You suddenly move at incredible speed, suddenly appearing next to a foe*
- Effect: Move to a space adjacent to a Foe within your [[Ranges#^564a37|Pathable Range]] that you can see. Your next action gains the [[Piercing Trait]] and the [[Surprising Trait]]. You may spend an extra AP to Push them 5 spaces upward, you may choose to maintain your relative position to them.
- [[Move Trait|Move]] [[Charge Trait|Charge]] [[Poly Trait|Poly]]
#### Combo Actions:
##### Tail Wind, 1 AP
- *You are so powerful that your attacks unleash powerful winds*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Attack Trait]] and the [[Melee Trait]]
- Effect: Target one of the Foes targeted by your last action, deal 1d8 damage to them. This action gains the [[Fling Trait]] with a value equal to the damage it dealt.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Fling Trait|Fling]]
##### No Escape, 1 AP
- *After throwing away the enemy you follow after the to deliver a follow up attack.*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Fling Trait]] or the [[Push Trait]]
- Effect: You may move to adjacent space to a Foe you moved with your previous action.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Crushing Trait|Crushing]]
##### Beatdown, 1 AP
- *You focus on the battle*
- Condition: Your last action Afflicted a Foe with the [[Impacted Curse]]
- Effect: Deal 1d6 damage, deal an extra 1d4 damage for every stack of the [[Impacted Curse]] they have.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]]
##### Epochal return, 1 AP
- *After taking a small pause you return to crush your enemies*
- Condition: Your last had the [[Move Trait]]
- Effect: Move back to the space you started moving from with your previous action. Your next action with the [[Poly Trait]] has its AP cost **lowered** to its original cost after you increase it.
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Revel in your power, 2 AP
- *You revel in the power you possess*
- Condition: Your last action dealt Overflowing damage.
- Effect: Gain stacks of the [[Energized Blessing]] equal to the Overflowing damage. If the Overflowing damage is greater than 10 you additionally gain a stack of [[Adrenaline]]
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]

#### Drive Actions:
##### Iron Skin
- *Just as a foe is about to strike you, you channel your power to fortify yourself.*
- Condition: a Foe targets you with an attack.
- Effect: gain 2d6 stacks of the [[Protected Blessing]] before taking damage from the triggering attack
- [[Defend Trait|Defend]]
##### Immovable Object
- *You resist others' attempts to push you around*
- Condition: you would incur forced movement
- Effect: You are not moved by the forced movement. If your speed is greater than the speed of the moving action you gain [[Adrenaline]]
- [[Defend Trait|Defend]]
##### Limit Break, 7 AP
- *You've pushed past the limits of your body, now it's time to push past the limits of Exceed itself.*
- Effect: Consume all stacks of [[Drive]], gain one stack of [[Adrenaline|Adrenaline]] for each stack. Until the end of the Scene any damage you deal is increased by 1d6 for every 3 stacks of [[Adrenaline|Adrenaline]] you have.
- [[Final Trait|Final]]
