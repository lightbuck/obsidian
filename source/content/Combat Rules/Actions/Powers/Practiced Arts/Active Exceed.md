#### Actions:
##### Mega Fist, 4 AP
- *You charge forward, destroying anything in your path*
- Effect: Target a Foe, deal 6d8 damage.
- [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
##### Siege Engine, 2 AP
- *You charge forward, destroying anything in your path*
- Effect: Move in a line for double your speed. Destroy any cover in your path. Foes adjacent to you at any point during this movement gain a stack of the [[Impacted Curse]] if they didn't have any.
- [[Move Trait|Move]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
##### Earth Shaker, 2 AP
- *You slam a Foe into the ground*
- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 2d6 damage. If the Target already had the [[Impacted Curse]] you deal an extra 1d8 damage. 
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Fling Trait|Fling(10)]]
##### Burst Dash, 1 AP
- *You suddenly move at incredible speed, suddenly appearing next to a foe*
- Effect: Move to a space adjacent to a Foe within your [[Ranges#^564a37|Pathable Range]] that you can see. Your next action gains the [[Piercing Trait]] and the [[Surprising Trait]].
- [[Move Trait|Move]] [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Launch, 2 AP
- *You suddenly move at incredible speed, suddenly appearing next to a foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 2d6 damage. Push the Target upward an amount of spaces equal to the damage this action dealt.
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Push Trait|Push]]
#### Combo Actions:
##### Tail Wind, 1 AP
- *You are so powerful that your attacks unleash powerful winds*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Attack Trait]] and the [[Melee Trait]]
- Effect: Target one of the Foes targeted by your last action, deal 1d8 damage to them. This action gains the [[Fling Trait]] with a value equal to the damage it dealt.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] 
##### No Escape, 1 AP
- *After throwing away the enemy you follow after the to deliver a follow up attack.*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Fling Trait]] or the [[Push Trait]]
- Effect: You may move to adjacent space to a Foe you moved with your previous action.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Crushing Trait|Crushing]]
##### Grave Digger, 1 AP
- *You focus on the battle*
- Condition: Your last action Afflicted a Foe with the [[Impacted Curse]]
- Effect: Target the same Foe as the last action, deal 1d8 damage to them. If they are not on top of a surface both you and the Foe Fall after resolving this action, but you don't take any damage from this Fall.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]]
##### Epochal return, 1 AP
- *After taking a small pause you return to crush your enemies*
- Condition: Your last had the [[Move Trait]]
- Effect: Move back to the space you started moving from with your previous action. Your next action with the [[Poly Trait]] has its AP cost **lowered** to its original cost after you increase it.
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Revel in your power, 2 AP
- *You revel in the power you possess*
- Condition: Your last action dealt Overflowing damage.
- Effect: Your next action gains the [[Ensured Trait]]. If the Overflowing damage is greater than 10 you additionally gain a stack of [[Adrenaline]]
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]

#### Drive Actions:
##### Unstoppable Force
- *Just as a foe is about to strike you, you channel your power to fortify yourself.*
- Effect: Until the end of the Round gain +5 speed, you are able to move through [[The Battlefield#Cover|Cover]] the GM deems breakable, destroying it as you do so.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Parallel Trait|Parallel]]
##### Iron Skin
- *Just as a foe is about to strike you, you channel your power to fortify yourself.*
- Effect: Take no damage from an incoming Attack.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Counter Trait|Counter]]
##### Shattering Resolve
- *You resist others' attempts to push you around*
- Effect: Your next action gain the [[Piercing Trait]], if it already had it then it gains the [[Brutal Trait]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
##### **Beyond**
- *You've pushed past the limits of your body, now it's time to push past the limits of Exceed itself.*
- Effect: Consume all stacks of [[Drive]] remaining, gain one stack of [[Adrenaline|Adrenaline]] for each stack. Until the end of the Scene any damage you deal is increased by 1d6 for every 3 stacks of [[Adrenaline|Adrenaline]] you have.
- [[Final Trait|Final]] [[Driven Trait|Driven]]
