This is due to the fact that Mana Scripting is not an embodied form of magic. But instead it consists in writing spells with liquid Mana, much like one would write high level computer code. These spells often consist of simple matter and energy generation, but in combat *

# Special
## Schools
When gaining this power, choose up to two schools, which represent which spells you have learned to write.
### School of Devastation
The school of devastation is the hardest school to master, it revolves around the precise utilization of energy to bring upon destruction.
#### Combo Actions:
##### Fire, 1 AP
- *You cast a simple yet reliable spell: a ball of fire*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Radius of 1 space, deal 2d6 damage to all Foes within it. Additionally you gain 1d4 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]]
##### Blizzard, 2 AP
- *You summon a spike of ice and send it flying forward*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a line starting from you, with a width of 2 spaces and length of 6 spaces, deal 2d6 damage to all Foes within it. Additionally you gain 2d4 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]]
##### Thunder, 2 AP
- *You unleash lightning before you wildly*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Cone angled from you of 4 spaces, deal 1d4 damage to all Foes within it. Additionally you gain 1d4 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]] [[Piercing Trait|Piercing]]
##### Flare, 2 AP
- *You unleash a blast of pure heat and light*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Radius of 3 spaces, deal 3d10 damage to all Characters within it. Foes hit by this attack gain a stack of the [[Impacted Curse]]. Additionally gain 2d10+2 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]]
##### Meteor, 2 AP
- *You unleash a blast of pure heat and light*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Radius of 5 spaces, deal 4d8 damage to all Characters within it. Foes hit by this attack gain a stack of the [[Impacted Curse]]. Additionally you gain 3d8+4 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].

### School of Evocation
The school of Evocation revolves around summoning matter.
#### Combo Actions:
##### Inflict H Beam, 2 AP
- *You summon a large metal H Beam and unceremoniously drop it on your foes.*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a line starting from you, with a width of 1 spaces and length of 8 spaces, deal 1d8 damage to all Characters within it and they must move to an adjacent space not within the Area. The Area then becomes [[The Battlefield#Partial Cover|Partial Cover]]. Additionally you gain 1d8 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Area Trait|Area]] 
##### Wall of Stone, 2 AP
- *You summon a solid wall of stone*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Line of width 1 and length 3, these spaces must be unoccupied. Turn the targeted spaces into [[The Battlefield#Full Cover|Full Cover]]. Additionally you gain 1d6 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Area Trait|Area]]
##### Gust of Gas, 2 AP
- *You summon a cloud of corrosive Gas*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Radius of 2 spaces, enemies are pushed from the center outward by 3 spaces. Afterward, the targeted area remains until its area is overlapped by another [[Area Trait|Area]] Action. Foes that end their turn in the Area gain the [[Impacted Curse]]. Additionally you gain 1d8 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Area Trait|Area]] 
##### Frozen Field, 2 AP
- *You freeze the floor ahead of your*
- Condition: Your previous action had the [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Radius of 4 spaces, Foes within the targeted spaces gain the [[Immobilized Hex]]. Afterward, the targeted area remains until its area is overlapped by another [[Area Trait|Area]] Action. Characters taking a move action within the Area gain the [[Impacted Curse]]. Additionally you gain 1d8 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Area Trait|Area]]
### School of the Glyphant
The school of the Glyphant revolves around the usage of Glyphs, usually the holographic symbol of a Spell made out of the Mana used to cast it, except to use them for visual magics, temporary barriers and to exert kinetic force. 
#### Actions:
##### Abracadabra, 1 AP
- *You cast a simple yet reliable spell: a ball of fire*
- Effect: Target either a Character, an ongoing Area, or a piece of cover that occupies up to 8 spaces. In the latter two cases the area or the piece of cover must either have been created by you or the GM agrees that it can be moved. Move the piece of cover by 3 spaces for each AP spent on this action. Characters on this piece of Cover are moved with it. Additionally you gain 1d4 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Poly Trait|Poly]]
##### Alakazam, 1 AP
- *You cast a simple yet reliable spell: a ball of fire*
- Effect: Target either a piece of cover that occupies up to 4 spaces. The piece of cover must either have been created by you or the GM agrees can be moved. Move the piece of cover by 2 spaces for each AP spent on this action. Characters that overlap with the cover at any point of its movement choose whether to take 1d6 damage or gain 1 stack of the [[Impacted Curse]]. Additionally you gain 2d4 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Poly Trait|Poly]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]]
##### Hocus Pocus, 1 AP
- *You cast a simple yet reliable spell: a ball of fire*
- Effect: Gain the [[Flighted Blessing]] and 5 stacks of the [[Energized Blessing]]. Move two spaces upward. Additionally you gain 1d4 stacks of the [[Execution Curse]].
- [[Move Trait|Move]]
##### Bibbidi, 1 AP
- *You cast a simple yet reliable spell: a ball of fire*
