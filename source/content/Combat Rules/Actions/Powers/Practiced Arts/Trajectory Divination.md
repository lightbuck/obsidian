##### Preammuntion, 2 AP
- *You load special ammunition which you have used before in the process of creating a prophecy.*
- Effect: Your next Attack with the [[Ranged Trait]] targets all Foes in a line instead,  its width is of 1 and its length is equal to half of the damage rolled. The line starts from the space of the Foe the Action would normally target and has the same direction as the line of sight between you and that Foe. That action also counts as having the [[Area Trait]] for the purposes of Combo Actions' Conditions.
