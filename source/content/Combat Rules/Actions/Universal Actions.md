There are some actions that are available to any any character
##### Move, 1 AP
- *You move.*
- Effect: Move a number of spaces equal to your speed.
- [[Move Trait|Move]]
##### Aid, 1 AP
- *You help an Ally.*
- Effect: Remove a negative condition from an Ally.
- [[Aid Trait|Aid]]
##### Brace, 1 AP
- *You brace yourself.*
- Effect: Roll your recovery die twice and pick a result.
- [[Defend Trait|Defend]]
##### Hit, 1 AP
- *You hit a Foe.*
- Effect: Target a Foe, deal 1d4 damage.
- [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
##### Throw, 1 AP
- *You throw something at a Foe.*
- Effect: Target a Foe, deal 1d4 damage.
- [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
##### Interact, 1 AP
- *You interact with something.*
- Effect: Interact in some way with something on [[The Battlefield]]. The AP cost of this action is determined by the GM.
-  -
##### Swap, 1 AP
- *You change your Armaments.*
- Effect: Swap an Equipped Armament for another in your possession. The cost of this Action is determined by the Armament you are swapping to. This action may cost 0 AP.
