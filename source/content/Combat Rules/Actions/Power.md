Power is gained by fulfilling your [[Roles|Role's]] duties. And it can be expended to do one of two things, [[Trait Transfer#Flexing A Trait|Flexing a Trait]],  improving Actions or removing Wounds.
# Flexing A Trait
Consuming two Stacks of [[Power]], to temporarily make so that the previous or the next [[Actions|Action]] gains a trait for this turn. 
# Power Boost
By spending power you can temporarily boost an action, increasing its damage or other benefits gained. This system is dynamic and  it is up to the GM what you can increase or not. Here are a few examples of how it works, use these as guidelines and extrapolate from them !
- Increasing size: spend one power to increase the size of [[Area Trait|Areas]] by  one
- Increasing damage: spend one power to increase the die size of one roll within an action's effects (for example, an action deals 3d4 and 2d6 damage to two different targets, increasing it would mean the rolls become 3d6 and 2d6, or 3d4 and 2d8).
- Increase speed: spend one power to increase any movement you make by your speed.
- Increasing Traits: spend one power to increase the value within the brackets of a trait by one. (such as gain(3) becomes gain(4))
- Increase Aid: spend a point of empowered to remove an extra stack of  a negative condition you were already removing.
The GM should avoid allowing Power to be used to increase the gained positive conditions by Effects of actions. For example an action's effect allows a character to gain 1 point of [[Adrenaline]], you should not be able to spend Power to increase that effect. 
# Power through
A player character may use their power to remove a wound, spending 3 points of it.
