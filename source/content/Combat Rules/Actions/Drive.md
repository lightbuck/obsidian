Drive is gained by fulfilling your [[Roles|Role's]] duties, and it is the main way you expand the options available to you! In practice it can be expended to do one of several things:
- Recovering a Wound
- Empowering an Action
- Temporarily adding Traits to  actions
- Getting help from an Ally
# Drive Recovery
A player character may spend 2 Drive to remove a Wound. You may also do this on an adjacent Ally.
# Drive Boost
By spending Drive you can temporarily boost an action, increasing its damage, healing or the values of its traits or other values within its Effect.
- Increasing size: spend one Drive to increase the size of [[Area Trait|Areas]] by  one
- Increasing damage: spend one Drive to increase the die size of one roll within an action's effects (for example, an action deals 3d4 and 2d6 damage to two different targets, increasing it would mean the rolls become 4d4 and 2d6, or 3d4 and 3d6).
- Increase speed: spend one Drive to increase any movement you take by your speed.
- Increasing Traits: spend one Drive to increase the value within the brackets of a trait by one. (such as gain(3) becomes gain(4))
- Increase Aid: spend a point of empowered to remove an extra stack of  a negative condition you were already removing.
The GM should avoid allowing Drive to be used to increase the gained positive conditions by Effects of actions. For example an action's effect allows a character to gain 1 point of [[Adrenaline]], you should not be able to spend Drive to increase that effect. 
# Drive Trick
You can consume a stack of [[Drive]], to temporarily add a Trait to the next Action you take. You must already have access to this Trait and it must make sense for the Action. For example adding an [[Attack Trait]] to an action that does not deal damage does nothing. But adding the [[Mobile Trait]] would work. While adding the [[Move Trait]] would not. 
A general rule of thumb is if the trait you are adding changes or improves something the action is already doing then it will work. While if you are trying to add something new to the action it likely won't.
# Drive Tag
Consume two Stacks of [[Drive]] use an Ally's [[Crossover Trait|Crossover]] Action
