Adrenaline is used to reduce an action's AP cost by 1 to a minimum of 1, consuming an equal amount of Adrenaline.
Gain a stack of Adrenaline every time your total amount of [[Drive]] consumed reaches one of these thresholds:
- 3 stacks
- 5 stacks
- 8 stacks
- 9 stacks
These intervals exist to ensure that combat ramps up at a moderate pace. After these intervalls.
