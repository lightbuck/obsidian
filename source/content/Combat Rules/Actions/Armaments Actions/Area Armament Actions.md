#### Main Actions:
##### Burst, 2 AP
- *You unleash a wide attack in front of you.*
- Effect: Target a Cone angled from you of 4 spaces, deal 1d6 damage to all Foes within it.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]] 
##### Blast, 2 AP
- *You explode an area of the battlefield.*
- Effect: Target a Radius of 2 spaces, deal 1d6 damage to all Foes within it.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]]
##### Eruption, 2 AP
- *You unleash an attack all around you.*
- Effect: Target a Radius of 2 spaces centered on you, deal 1d8 damage to all Foes within it.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]]
##### Beam, 1 AP
- *You attack all that stand before you.*
- Effect: Target a line starting from you, with a width of 2 spaces and length of 4 spaces, deal 1d4 damage to all Foes within it.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]]
#####  Eye For Sizes, 1 AP
- *You study the battlefield to better make use of your attack.*
- Effect: The next action that has the [[Area Trait]] has increased size by one.
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]] 
##### Careful Demolisher, 2 AP
- *You study the battlefield to better focus your attacks.*
- Effect: The next action that has the [[Area Trait]] has decreased size by one, it also deals an extra point of damage for each space it no longer targets.
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]] 
##### Propelling Flare , 1 AP
- *You push past your foes with a beam.*
- Effect: Take a move action in a line and target a line of width 3 from the start of your movement to where it ended. Deal 1d4 damage to all Foes.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]] [[Move Trait|Move]]
##### Blasting Leap, 1 AP
- *You leap away with a blast*
- Effect: Target a Radius of 2 spaces. Deal 1d4 damage to all Foes and then take a Move Action.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]] [[Move Trait|Move]]
##### Opening Blast, 2 AP
- *You blast a Foe, opening them up for further attacks*
- Effect: Target a Radius of 2 spaces, deal 1d4 damage to all Foes within it. Choose one of the Targeted Foes, until they take damage to health, any action without the [[Area Trait]] that targets them deals an extra 1d6 damage. 
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]

#### Combo Actions: 
##### The Bigger The Better, 1 AP
- *You make your attack even larger.*
- Condition: your last action had [[Charge Trait]]
- Effect: The next action that has the [[Area Trait]] has increased size by one.
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]] 
##### Closing Blast, 1 AP
- *You deliver a final strike with your Armament.*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Radius of 2 spaces, deal 1d4 damage to all Foes within it for every action you took this turn that had the [[Attack Trait]] but not the [[Area Trait]]. 
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]] [[Consumed Trait|Consumed]]

#### Reactions: 
##### Beam-O-War, R
- *You stop an Area attack with one of your own*
- Condition: You are targeted with an [[Area Trait|Area]] Attack
- Effect:   Take a 1 AP Attack with the [[Area Trait]] targeting the Triggering Foe.
- [[Defend Trait|Defend]]
