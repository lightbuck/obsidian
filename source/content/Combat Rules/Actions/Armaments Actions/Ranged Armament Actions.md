#### Main Actions:
##### Quick Fire, 1 AP
- *You shoot a Foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe that you can see, deal 1d6 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
##### Snipe, 2 AP
- *You shoot a Foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe that you can see, deal 4d4 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Immobile Trait|Immobile]]
##### Precise Aim, 2 AP
- *You focus and aim at a target.*
- Effect: Target one Foe, the next Attack with the [[Ranged Trait]] against the Targeted Foe gains the [[Brutal Trait]] and deals an extra 2d4 damage.
- [[Immobile Trait|Immobile]] [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Curved Shot, 2 AP
- *You shoot a Foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe that is within your [[Ranges#Pathable Range|Pathable Range]] , deal 1d6 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Immobile Trait]]
##### Double Shot, 1 AP
- *You shoot a Foe*
- Effect: Target two Foes that you can see, deal 1d4 damage to both.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
##### Disengaging Shot, 1 AP
- *You shoot a Foe*
- Effect: Target a Foe that's adjacent to you, deal 1d6 damage to them and take a move action in a line, in the opposite direction of them. 
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Move Trait|Move]]
##### Opening Shot, 2 AP
- *You shoot a Foe, opening them up for further attacks*
- Effect: Target one Foe that you can see, deal 1d4 damage. Until the Targeted Foe takes damage to health, any action without the [[Ranged Trait]] that targets them deals an extra 1d6 damage. 
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]


#### Combo Actions: 
##### Reload, 1 AP
- *You reload your armament.*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Ranged Trait]] and the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Your next [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] Attack deals an extra 1d6 damage.
-  [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Closing Shot, 1 AP
- *You deliver a final strike with your Armament.*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Foe that you can see, deal a 1d6 of damage for every action you took this turn that had the [[Attack Trait]] but not the [[Ranged Trait]]. 
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Consumed Trait|Consumed]]
