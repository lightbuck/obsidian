#### Main Actions:
##### Shotgun Burst, 2 AP
- *You unleash a wide attack in front of you.*
- Effect: Target a Cone angled from you of 4 spaces, deal 1d6 damage to all Foes within it.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Multi Trait|Multi (Melee, Area)]]
##### Shotgun Leap, 1 AP
- *You leap away with a blast*
- Effect: Target a Cone angled from you of 3 spaces. Deal 1d4 damage to all Foes and then take a Move Action.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Move Trait|Move]] [[Multi Trait|Multi (Melee, Area)]]
##### Stock Slam, 1 AP
- *You slam a Foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 1d6 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Crushing Trait|Crushing]] [[Multi Trait|Multi (Melee, Area)]]
##### Shotgun Opening, 2 AP
- *You blast a Foe, opening them up for further attacks*
- Effect: Target a Cone angled from you of 3 spaces, deal 1d4 damage to all Foes within it. Choose one of the Targeted Foes, until they take damage to health, any action without the [[Area Trait]] that targets them deals an extra 1d6 damage. 
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]] [[Multi Trait|Multi (Melee, Area)]]

#### Combo Actions: 
##### Load Shell, 1 AP
- *You rack a shell out of your Armament.*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Melee Trait]] or the [[Area Trait]], and the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Your next attack with either the [[Melee Trait]] or the [[Area Trait]] deals an extra 1d8 damage.
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Stock Burst, 2 AP
- *Fire your Armament to use its kickback to hit the opponent harder.*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Charge Trait]].
- Effect: Target a Foe within two spaces of you, move in a line to be adjacent to them. Deal 2d6 Damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Surprising Trait|Surprising]] [[Move Trait|Move]] [[Multi Trait|Multi (Melee, Area)]]
#### Drive Actions: 
##### Pump and slide
- *You quickly rack a shot as you slide toward a Foe.*
- Effect: Take a move action toward a foe that you can see, your next attack gains the [[Surprising Trait]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
##### Piercing Buckshot
- *Load into you Armament a more precise round.*
- Effect: The next action that has the [[Area Trait]] with the same armament as this action, has its Area replaced to a Line of length 6 and width of one. It also gains the [[Surprising Trait]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
