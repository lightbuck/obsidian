#### Main Actions:
##### Raise Defenses, 2 AP
- *You prepare yourself for incoming attacks*
- Effect: Roll your recovery Die twice, gain any overflowing stamina as stacks of the [[Protected Blessing]].
- [[Defend Trait|Defend]]
##### Stand with me, 2 AP
- *You offer protection to an Ally*
- Effect: Target an adjacent Allied Character. The next time the target would gain a condition, they don't gain it if they are adjacent to you.
- [[Defend Trait|Defend]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
##### Hard to miss, 1 AP
- *You offer protection to an Ally*
- Effect: The next Attack a foe makes from an adjacent space, or the next Ranged attack that passes through an adjacent space, is Re-Targeted to you.
- [[Defend Trait|Defend]]

#### Combo Actions: 
##### Share defenses, 1 AP
- *You inspire a nearby ally to prepare themselves*
- Condition: your last action made you gain the [[Protected Blessing]]
- Effect: Target an adjacent Ally, they gain half the amount of stacks of the [[Protected Blessing]] that you gained.
- [[Defend Trait|Defend]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
##### I'll cover you!, 1 AP
- *You yell out to an Ally to move*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Aid Trait]] or the [[Defend Trait]] and targeted an Ally
- Effect: That Ally gains 1d6 stacks of the [[Protected Blessing]] a move action.
-  [[Defend Trait|Defend]]

#### Drive Actions: 
##### Get behind me!
- *You defend an Ally*
- Effect: An Ally that you can see takes no damage from an attack that is targeting them, move next to them and Re-Target the triggering Attack to you
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Counter Trait|Counter]]
