##### Find Vulnerability
- *You spot a weakness in your Foe's defenses.*
- Effect:  Chose a Foe that you can see, your next action if it is an attack against them gains the [[Piercing Trait]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
##### Proclaim Weakness
- *You find your Foe's vulnerability and proclaim it for your allies to exploit as well.*
- Effect: Target a Foe, they gain the [[Hunted Curse]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
##### Read Opponent
- *You learn of your opponent's weakness and strengths as you fight.*
- Effect: For the rest of the round and time you target a Foe with an action you may ask the GM for a piece of information on the Foe's statblock at the beginning of the action. Additionally Once per Round you may choose to cancel an Action you were about to take due to the new information, however doing this ends this effect early. 
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]] [[Parallel Trait|Parallel]] [[Counter Trait|Counter]]
