##### Disarming Strikes
- *You set yourself to disarm a Foe.*
- Effect: Your next attack Afflicts one Foe whose stamina it exhausts with the [[Disarmed Curse]].

##### Disarming Impacts
- *You set yourself to disarm a Foe.*
- Effect: If your next action Afflicts a Foe with the [[Impacted Curse]] it also Afflicts the [[Disarmed Curse]] to that one Foe.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
