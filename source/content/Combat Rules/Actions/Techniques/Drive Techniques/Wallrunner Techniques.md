##### Wall Dash
- *Description.*
- Effect: Any movement you take with your next Action is increased by half if it starts adjacent to a vertical surface. Alternatively if it ends adjacent to a vertical surface you may increase it then, extending it even if it technically ended already. That action also gains the [[Surprising Trait]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
##### Wall Run
- *Description.*
- Effect: For this Round you increase your speed by 5, when beginning to move adjacent only to vertical surfaces. Actions taken while in that state also gain the [[Ensured Trait]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Parallel Trait|Parallel]]
##### Wall Hang
- *Description.*
- Effect: Cancel Falling if you are at least within 1 space of a vertical surface, then move so that you are adjacent to it. You gain Stamina equal to your Recovery Die. Using this [[Counter Trait|Counter]] Action does not make you Fall after it is resolved.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Counter Trait|Counter]]
