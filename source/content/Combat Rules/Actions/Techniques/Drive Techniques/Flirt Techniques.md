##### Blow Kiss
- *You blow a kiss to someone, either inspiring them or making them mad but distracting them either way.*
- Effect: Target a **Character**, they may gain 1d10 stacks of the [[Boosted Blessing]] and a stack of the [[Impacted Curse]]. **Foes** that opt not to gain these effects instead gain the following [[Behaviours|Behaviour]]: "Spent at least 1 AP moving away from [Character]" where in [Character] is you.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
##### Turn the tables
- *You surprise a Foe about to strike you with a flirtatuous move, distracting them from hurting you.*
- Effect: Afflict a Foe that is Attacking you, with one stack of the [[Impacted Curse]], that Attack gains the [[Mental Trait]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Counter Trait|Counter]]
