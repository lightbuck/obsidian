#WIP
Wrestler Techniques give characters flexible and damaging moves with the [[Poly Trait]] with some afflictions.
Recommended Armaments: [[Melee Armament]]
#### Main Actions:
##### Slide Tackle, 1 AP
- *You charge forward, destroying anything in your path*
- Effect: Move in a line for double your speed. If during this movement you become adjacent to a Foe, you may choose to spend an extra AP and end the movement at that point and Target that Foe. The Target takes 1d8 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Poly Trait|Poly]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Move Trait|Move]]
##### Bell Ringer, 1 AP
- *You slam a Foe into the ground*
- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 1d6 damage. If the Target already had the [[Impacted Curse]] or would gain it because of this action, you deal an extra 1d6 damage. You may spend an extra AP to add the [[Fling Trait]] with a value of 5 to this action.
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Poly Trait|Poly]]
#### Combo Actions:
##### Caught and Crashed, 1 AP
- *After throwing away the enemy you follow after the to deliver a follow up attack.*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Fling Trait]] or the [[Push Trait]] and its target was adjacent to you.
- Effect: The target of the triggering action gains the [[Impacted Curse]] and is repositioned to a space adjacent to you.
- [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
##### Beatdown, 1 AP
- *You focus on the battle*
- Condition: Your last action Afflicted a Foe with the [[Impacted Curse]]
- Effect: Deal 1d6 damage, deal an extra 1d4 damage for every stack of the [[Impacted Curse]] they have.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]]
