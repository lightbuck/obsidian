A Technique based around swapping Armaments and granting bonuses for doing so.
Recommended Armaments: At least 3 Armaments of any type, except Armaments who can't be drawn such as Armaments with the [[Inherent Aspect]] or [[Companion Armament|Companion Armaments]]
#### Main Actions:
##### Devil May Juggle, 1 AP
- *You ready yourself to swap between your armaments.*
- Effect: For the duration of this Round any time you use an Armament you haven't used this Round yet, the first Attack with it gains the [[Surprising Trait]]. If you make use of at least three different Armaments by the end of this Round, you restart the effect the next Round for free, this can be done so indefinetely.
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Quick Strike, 1 AP
- *You quickly pull out an Armament to strike a Foe.*
- Effect: Target a Foe, deal 1d10 damage. If this is the first Action you take with the currently equipped Armament this Round this action deals an extra 1d4 damage.
- [[Melee Trait|Melee]]  [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
##### Sleek Shot, 1 AP
- *You quickly pull out an Armament to strike a Foe.*
- Effect: Target one Foe that you can see, deal 1d8 damage. If this is the first Action you take with the currently equipped Armament this Round this action deals an extra 1d4 damage.
- [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
##### Pocket Burst, 1 AP
- *You quickly pull out an Armament to strike a Foe.*
- Effect: Target a radius of 1 space, deal 1d8 damage to all Characters in the Area. If this is the first Action you take with the currently equipped Armament this Round this action deals an extra 1d4 damage.
- [[Area Trait|Area]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
##### Arsenal Showcase, 2 AP
- *You quickly swap between weapons as you strike your foes.*
- Effect: Take a 1 AP Main Action or useable Combo Action (checking the previous Action for its condition) with the [[Attack Trait]], afterward you may swap Armaments. Repeat this Effect once but you may not use the same 1 AP Action than once.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
#### Combo Actions: 
##### Insulting Holstering, 1 AP
- *You make a big show of holstering your weapon, wounding your Foe's Ego.*
- Condition: Your last action made use of an Armament
- Effect: Holster the Armament used by the Triggering Action, without Drawing another Armament. Then Target the same Foe as your previous Action, deal 2d6 Damage to them.
- [[Mental Trait|Mental]]
##### Drawn To You, 1 AP
- *You wink at, grin at or otherwise menace a Foe you are currently fighting while you swap your weapons.*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Target the same Foe as the Triggering Action, they gain the [[Hunted Curse]]. Additionally Swap Armaments.
- [[Afflict Trait]] 
#### Drive Actions: 
##### Moving Arsenal
- *Description.* #PH
- Effect: Until the end of the Round, any time you Swap weapons you may [[Step Trait|Step with a value of 3]].
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Parallel Trait|Parallel]]
