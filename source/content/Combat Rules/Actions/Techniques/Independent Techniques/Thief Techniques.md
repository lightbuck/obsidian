Description
Recommended Armaments:
#### Main Actions:
##### Hide, 1 AP
- *You hide.*
- Effect: gain the [[Hidden Blessing]].
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Pickpocket, 2 AP
- *Description.*
- Effect: Remove up to 6 stacks of a Blessing from an adjacent Foe, if you had the [[Hidden Blessing]] when you took this action you gain the stacks you removed and this action gains the [[Charge Trait]].
- [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Step Trait|Step(2)]]
##### Grand Theft Armament, 2 AP
- *Description.*
- Effect: Target an adjacent Foe, if they have the [[Vulnerable Curse]] you may take an [[Armaments|Armament]] without the [[Inherent Aspect]] from the Target. You may immediately swap to it and at the GM's discretion you may gain access to one action that your Foe takes with that Armament. You drop the stolen Armament (in an adjacent space chosen by the GM) if you take damage to health or gain the [[Impacted Curse]].
- [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Step Trait|Step(2)]]
##### Trip up, 1 AP
- *You hide.*
- Effect: Target an adjacent Foe, Afflict them with one stack of the [[Impacted Curse]]. If you had the [[Hidden Blessing]] when you took this action you Afflict two stacks of the [[Impacted Curse]] instead.
- [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Step Trait|Step(2)]]
##### Smoke Bomb, 3 AP
- *You throw a smoke Bomb.*
- Effect: Target a Radius of 4, all characters in that Radius gain the [[Hidden Blessing]], but they may only place themselves back on the board in a space within or adjacent to that Area. The Area itself persists until it is overlapped with another Area and it provides [[The Battlefield#Partial Cover|Partial Cover]].
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
#### Combo Actions: 
##### Flashy Reappearance, 1 AP
- *Description.*
- Condition: You have the [[Hidden Blessing]].
- Effect: Remove the [[Hidden Blessing]] and place yourself back on The Battlefield.  All foes adjacent to you take 1d6 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Surprising Trait|Surprising]] [[Mental Trait|Mental]]
##### Menace, 1 AP
- *Description.*
- Condition: You have the [[Hidden Blessing]].
- Effect:  Target a Foe, they gain the [[Hunted Curse]]. 
- [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
##### Fear Takedown, 2 AP
- *Description.*
- Condition: Your last action Knocked Out a Foe or made them gain the [[Vulnerable Curse]].
- Effect: Gain the [[Hidden Blessing]]. Remove that Foe's token from the battlefield, deal 1 damage to their Health. If the Target was knocked out because of that damage, or was already knocked out, the three closest Foes that had line of sight with them gain the [[Impacted Curse]]. If the Foe is not knocked out they gain the [[Hidden Blessing]], but they may only place themselves back on the board on the Space where they were before.
- [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
##### Hit and Run, 1 AP
- *Description.*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Attack Trait]].
- Effect: Gain the [[Hidden Blessing]], roll your recovery Die twice and pick the better result.
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
#### Drive Actions: 
##### Penumbral Dip
- *Description.*
- Effect: If your last action made you lose the [[Hidden Blessing]], you regain it at the end of your next action.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
##### Intercept #PH
- *Description.*
- Effect: You may take this action while you have the [[Hidden Blessing]], remove it if you have it. Cancel an attack directed at an Ally.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Counter Trait|Counter]]
##### Thief's Loyalty
- *Description.*
- Effect: You may take this action while you have the [[Hidden Blessing]], remove it if you have it. Retarget an Action that has the [[Afflict Trait]], and that was directed at one of your Allies, to yourself.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Counter Trait|Counter]]
