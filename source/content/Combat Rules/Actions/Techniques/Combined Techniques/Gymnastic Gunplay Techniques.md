These techniques aim to allow characters to make use of ranged weapons and melee attacks, supplementing them with high mobility and some afflictions.
Recommended Armaments: a [[Ranged Armament]] with a [[Melee Armament]] and either of them should have the [[Inherent Aspect]]. 
#### Main Actions:
##### Step Shot, 1 AP
- *You shoot a Foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe that you can see, deal 1d6 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Step Trait|Step(3)]]
##### Kickback, 1 AP
- *You kick away your Foe*
- Effect: Target a Foe, deal 1d6 damage to them. You may increase the cost of this action by 1 AP to increase the damage to 2d4 and increase this action's Fling Trait's Value to 7.
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Fling Trait|Fling(3)]] [[Poly Trait|Poly]]
##### Dive Kick, 1 AP
- *You slam a Foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe, move in a line up to your speed toward them and deal 1d6 damage. If your vertical position is higher than your target's when you take this action, this action gains the [[Surprising Trait]].
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Move Trait|Move]]
##### Opening Kick, 2 AP
- *You strike at a Foe, opening them up for further attacks*
- Effect: Target one Foe, deal 1d6 damage. Until the Targeted Foe takes damage to Health, any action with the [[Melee Trait]] deals an extra 1d6 damage and actions with the [[Ranged Trait]] deal an extra 1d8 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]]
#### Combo Actions: 
##### Recoiling Flip, 1 AP
- *You shoot a Foe*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Ranged Trait]] and the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Move in a line up to your speed, in the opposite direction of the Triggering Action's Target. Alternatively you may move in that direction with half your speed while moving half your speed upward. If you were adjacent to the Target when you took this action, they gain 1 stack of the [[Impacted Curse]] if they didn't have any.
- [[Move Trait|Move]] [[Defend Trait|Defend]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
##### Unorthodox Reload, 1 AP
- *You reload your armament while smacking your foe.*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Ranged Trait]] and the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Foe, deal 1d6 damage to them. Your next Attack with the [[Ranged Trait]] deals an extra 1d6 damage.
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Tripshot, 1 AP
- *You trip your foe with a well placed shot*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Push Trait]] or the [[Fling Trait]]
- Effect: Target the same Foe as the Triggering Action, they take 1d4 damage.
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Surprising Trait|Surprising]]
##### Closing Shot, 1 AP
- *You deliver a final strike with your Armament.*
- Condition: your last action had the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Target a Foe that you can see, deal a 1d6 of damage for every action you took this turn that had the [[Attack Trait]] but not the [[Ranged Trait]]. 
-  [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Ranged Trait|Ranged]] [[Consumed Trait|Consumed]]
#### Drive Actions:
##### Feint N Flip
- *You feint an attack, using the opening to move away*
- Effect: Your next attack gains the [[Mental Trait]], after resolving that action take up to two move actions, you may move through the spaces of the Foes targeted by that Action.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
