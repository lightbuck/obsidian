These techniques aim to allow Characters to make use of Thrown Weapons, meaning weapons that are both ranged and melee. It also has multiple instances of the [[Piercing Trait]].
Recommended Armaments: a [[Melee Armament]] or a [[Ranged Armament]]
#### Main Actions:
##### Double Throw, 1 AP
- *You throw your Armament at two Foes at once.*
- Effect: Target two Foes that you can see, deal 1d4 damage to both.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Multi Trait|Multi(Melee,Ranged)]] [[Mobile Trait|Mobile]]
##### Throw, 1 AP
- *You throw your Armament at a Foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe that you can see, deal 2d4 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Multi Trait|Multi(Melee,Ranged)]] [[Piercing Trait|Piercing]]
##### Flechette Storm, 1 AP
- *You throw your Armament at a Foe*
- Effect: Target a Foe and deal 1 damage to them. Repeat this effect two times (choosing a target every time). Additionally you may spend 1 extra AP on this action to repeat the effect two more times.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Multi Trait|Multi(Melee,Ranged)]] [[Poly Trait|Poly]]
##### Cut, 1 AP
- *You slash a Foe*
- Effect: Target one Foe adjacent to you, deal 1d8 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Multi Trait|Multi(Melee,Ranged)]] 
##### Cutting Advance, 1 AP
- *You move forward unleashing a series of attacks to anyone in your path*
- Effect: Target one Foe that you can see, move in a line up to your speed toward them, additionally target one other Foe that was adjacent to you during this movement. Both Foes take 1d6 damage.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Multi Trait|Multi(Melee,Ranged)]]
#### Combo Actions: 
##### Catch Weapon, 1 AP
- *Your weapon bounces off of your foe and you catch it in the air.*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Ranged Trait]] and the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Your next [[Multi Trait|Multi(Melee,Ranged)]] gains the [[Piercing Trait]] and deals an extra 1d4 damage.
- [[Move Trait|Move]] [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
##### Bounce, 1 AP
- *Description.*
- Condition: Your last action had the [[Ranged Trait]] and the [[Attack Trait]]
- Effect: Target another Foe within line of sight of the Triggering Action's Target. Deal 1d6 damage, up to the damage of the Triggering Action, to that Foe. This Action inherits any damage bonuses that applied to the Triggering Action.
- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Multi Trait|Multi(Melee,Ranged)]]
##### Flip Grip, 1 AP
- *Description.*
- Condition: Your last Action had the [[Ranged Trait]]
- Effect: Your next Attack with the [[Melee Trait]] deals an extra 2d4 damage and gains the [[Piercing Trait]].
- [[Charge Trait|Charge]]
#### Drive Actions: 
##### Curved Throw
- *Description.*
- Effect: Your next Action with the [[Ranged Trait]] may choose its targets from a space that you can see instead of your own position.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Woven Trait|Woven]]
##### Back Stabber
- *Description.*
- Effect: Target a foe that moves away from you with a 1 AP Main Action.
- [[Driven Trait|Driven]] [[Counter Trait|Counter]]
