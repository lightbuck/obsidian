# Movement Range

^21f354

Characters  "within your movement Range N" or "within N Move Range of you" are characters that you could be adjacent to if you took N Movement Actions.
# Pathable Range

Characters within "Your Pathable Range" are characters that you could, regardless of how many Movement Actions, be adjacent to.
# Line of Sight Range
Characters that you can draw a straight line from them to you unimpeded.
