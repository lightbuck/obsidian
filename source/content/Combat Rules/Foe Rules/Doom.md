Doom is a resource the GM gains when Foes follow their Behaviors, of from the failure condition of a [[Declarations|Declaration]].
The GM can spend Doom to throw in extra obstacles for the Players. 
When an NPC allied to the Players follows their behavior they instead give a random player Drive.
# Doom Dash
Take a move action with a minimum speed of 3.
# Doom Deny
Remove a condition from yourself.
