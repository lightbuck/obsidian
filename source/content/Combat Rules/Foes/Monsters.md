Monsters are different from most Human Foes, [[Game Phases#^5bb2cb|Declarations]] that cannot be countered through mere combat tactics but require something to be done roleplay wise to be rebuked.
## Size Classes
Monsters come in many size Classes
### Critters
Small Creatures that act together, usually composed only of [[Combat Rules/Foes/NPCs#^e3bd6a|Grunts]]
### Tale Beasts
Usually a single [[Combat Rules/Foes/NPCs#^4cc935|Heavy]] Foe, some may summon 'Minions', which are [[Combat Rules/Foes/NPCs#^e3bd6a|Grunts]]
### Maws
Usually a single [[Combat Rules/Foes/NPCs#^dbb12a|Captains]] Foe
### Leviathans
Foes that technically count as a [[Combat Rules/Foes/NPCs#^dbb12a|Captains]] Foe, but who cannot be damaged until they enter a weakened state. After entering their Weakened state Leviathans have different [[Game Phases#^5bb2cb|Declarations]] from before they entered the state. Before a Leviathan is weakened these [[Game Phases#^5bb2cb|Declarations]] often have the [[Piercing Trait|Piercing]] and [[Brutal Trait|Brutal Traits]]..
