Movement can happen in many different ways, a character can choose to move, they might have to move because of the effects of their actions, they could be flung by an action or they could fall.
# Falling
When in the air, or rather while not adjacent to any surface, Characters fall at the end of their turn. They take 1d6 damage for every 6 spaces they fell. 
## Hanging On
Characters do not begin to Fall, if they are adjacent to at least one vertical surface. However if the character takes damage to Health, gains the [[Impacted Curse]] or takes an action with the [[Counter Trait]] they will begin to Fall.
# Forced Movement
A character may be subjected to forced movement (such as being targeted by a an action with the [[Fling Trait]] or [[Push Trait]]). The character may endure some negatives effect depending on the result of movement.
## Hitting a surface
If the forced movement ends in such a way that it would continue were it not for a surface in the way, the character moved will gain the [[Impacted Curse]] up to 1 stack.
## Flying off
If the forced movement is greater than the affected character's speed, and it does not end in a surface, the character moved will gain the [[Impacted Curse]] up to 1 stack.
## Landing on your feet
if neither of the previous conditions apply the target will land on their feet and won't take any additional penalties.
