Characters have two hit point pools, stamina and health. Health instead represents their physical wellness.
# Stamina
Stamina represents a form active defense of a character, their ability to avoid, deflect or stop attacks.
Stamina is associated with a **Recovery Die**, which certain actions can invoke.
That is to say that a Player Character's pool of Stamina is equal to your Recovery Die's size. They can gain extra Stamina equal to the size of their **Stamina Pool**. Meaning what is known as their **Maximum Stamina** is equal to twice the value of their **Stamina Pool**, which certain actions can invoke.
When a Player Character enters combat their current stamina is equal to the value of their die, this value is referred to as their **Starting Stamina**, which certain actions can invoke.
# Health
Health is a measure of how many wounds a character can accumulate before they are unable to continue fighting.
A Player Character's Health is recovered 1 point at a time, for each scene in which they are not in danger. Alternatively they may have a scene dedicated to resting, in which they will recover up to two missing points of Health.
When a Player Character loses a point of health they make a Fight Forever Check.
## Fight Forever Checks
A player checks how many points of health their Character is missing and rolls a 1d6. If they roll above that number they gain a Wound, if they roll equal or below that number they gain a stack of [[Adrenaline]].
## Wounds
A wound temporarily denies access to an action. A Player Character gains a wound when they lose a point of health or at the start of combat, gaining a wound for each point of Health missing. When they gain a Wound the Player chooses which Action they lose access to. When they Recover a wound they DON'T Heal the corresponding point of Health missing. 
# Object Health
Physical Objects have health, which usually behaves differently from Character Health. Objects recover Health when they are fixed (which is done through a skill check) and they lose Health Points equal to the damage like a character's Stamina.
