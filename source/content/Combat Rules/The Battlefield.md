# Walls and Cover ^c3f85b
## Walls ^6e6320
A wall is something that exists within the environment that stops both movement and line of sight,
Walls can have different [[Health and Stamina#^227d2d|Object Health]] values, here are some examples:
- Wooden Planks: 4 Health
- Stone Pillar: 6 Health
- Steel Plates: 10 Health
- Energy Shield: 14 Health
- Subspace Matter: 30 Health
## Cover
A piece of Cover is something that stops line of sight in some way but does not impede movement. Pieces of Cover have [[Health and Stamina#^227d2d|Object Health]] like Walls, but usually halved from what a Wall would have, a flipped over Wooden Table would have 2 Health compared to a Wooden Wall, which would have 4 Health.
### Partial Cover ^909037
A piece of cove r is something that exists within the environment that stops line of sight for characters that aren't adjacent to it, but can be moved through.
### Full Cover ^4eb84d
A piece of cover is something that exists within the environment that stops line of sight for all characters but can be moved through.
# Surfaces
Surfaces are what characters are standing on or are [[Movement#Hanging On|Hanging On]] to. Sometimes they can have properties that hamper or facilitate movement, here's some examples:
- Slippery: Any character taking a movement action is forced to move an extra space in the direction of the last movement.
- Difficult: Moving from a space of this surface costs more speed than normal, the amount is rounded up.
- Painful: Characters take an amount of damage for ever space of this surface they move into. 
- Dangerous: Characters that end their turn on this surface take an amount of damage.
- Invigorating: Characters that end their turn on this surface gain an amount of [[Health and Stamina|Stamina]]
