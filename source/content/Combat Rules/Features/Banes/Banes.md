A bane is a flaw a character may have, something that they struggle with. Banes give access to a an action with the [[Bane Trait]], which MUST be equipped %%a #PH term %% . Bane actions usually cost 1 AP and inflict some sort of negative effect on your character, but they also allow you to gain[[Drive]].
# Player moderation
Banes are not meant to be ignored but they are meant to be a problem a character has to work around. The distinction is a fine line, so here is an example: A character has a bane which makes them unable to use the [[Mobile Trait]] on their actions, if the character however is a ranged based character and does not actually care about then they are ignoring the Bane. However the same Bane on a character that does need to move will be forced to make use of other types of movement, meaning they may plan to make use of things such as [[Drive#Drive Boost|Drive Boost]]. The latter is fine! 
As a Game Master it is your duty to make so that the former example is not allowed, all of the pre made Banes are built around avoiding situations like that example. But talk to your players if you see them attempt to ignore Banes. Remind them that a Bane is something their character should actively struggle with and they are not just a way to earn more [[Drive]].
# Creating Custom Banes
When making your own Bane for your players' characters, deciding the negative effect of the Bane Action can be tough to balance. It must be a significant negative effect that cannot be easily worked around, but it cannot be too negative. 
Examples of good effects:
- gaining 1 stack of the [[Impacted Curse]]
- Losing a stamina without taking damage to health
Examples of effects which are too negative:
- gaining the [[Disarmed Curse]] 
- Taking damage to health
- Reducing speed to 0

A bane that makes a Foe immediately gain [[Adrenaline]] is too strong ! but a bane that afflicts you with the [[Hunted Curse]] is acceptable!

**Other Considerations**
Effects such as a reduction to damage dealt do not affect suport based characters, meaning that the bane doesn't exist for them. So they should be paired along side Banes that reduce support capabilities, use the [[Weakened Hex]]! But also remember the earlier section on player moderation.
Bane actions with a higher cost than 1 AP may have smaller effects! But remember that Bane Actions cannot have their AP cost reduced.
