*This Armament is another entity, such as a drone or a wolf following you around and fighting by your side.*
Your companion exists as a token, sharing your Speed, Health and Stamina.
When you would take damage to your Health, because your Companion took damage, but you only have one point of Health left, you remove the companion from the battlefield instead and you don't take the damage nor you roll a [[Health and Stamina#Fight Forever Checks|Fight Forever Check]].
When you take a move you may spread the movement between you and your Companions. 
Add to any actions you have the [[Companion Trait]] with the name of this Armament.
Unlike other Armaments, you don't need to swap to it to use it. As long as it is on the battlefield you may move it.
**Cost**: 300 CR
