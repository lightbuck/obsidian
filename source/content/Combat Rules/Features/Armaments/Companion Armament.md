*This Armament is another entity, such as a drone or a wolf following you around and fighting by your side.*
Your companion exists as a token, sharing your speed Health and Stamina. When you take a move you may spread the movement between you and your Companions. Add to any actions you have the [[Companion Trait]] with the name of this Armament.
# Catalysts
This Armament may make use of up to two Catalysts

- [[Leaping Catalyst]]
- [[Winged Catalyst]]
- [[Levitating Catalyst]]
