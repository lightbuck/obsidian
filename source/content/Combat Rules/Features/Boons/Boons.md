Boons are positive aspects of your character, most can be acquired more than once but their cost increases depending on how many you wish to get. Some Boons will have a spike in their price early on, some increase linearly.
Each Boon costs a certain amount of Boon Points, and every character starts at 17 Boon Points.
# Types
## Basic Boons
Basic Boons can be acquired any amount of times, they cost starts at 1 points each. Basic Boons have tables to determine how their cost increases.
## Advanced Boons
Advanced Boons can be acquired up to 3 times, they cost 2 points each.
## Unique Boons
Unique Boons cost 3 points and can be acquired a single time.
