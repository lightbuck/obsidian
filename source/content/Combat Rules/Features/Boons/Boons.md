Boons are positive aspects of your character, most can be acquired more than once but their cost increases depending on how many you wish to get. Some Boons will have a spike in their price early on, some increase linearly.

# Combat Boons
## Types
### Basic Boons
Basic Boons can be acquired any amount of times, their cost starts at 1 point but increases by one for every purchase.
### Advanced Boons
Advanced Boons can be acquired up to three times, always costing 2 points each.
### Unique Boons
Unique Boons cost three points and can be acquired a single time.
### Dashing
Dashing Boons always only cost 1 point.
