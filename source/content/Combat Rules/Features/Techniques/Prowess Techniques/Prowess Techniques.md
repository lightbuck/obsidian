Prowess Techniques are Stylish Actions that grant small buffs, similar to [[To Power Techniques]] but weaker and always cost one Stack of the [[Empowered Condition]].
Players may work with the GM to create their own To Prowess Techniques, (with the understanding that these actions cannot have the [[Attack Trait]]). Here are some examples of

Main Actions:
- **Taunt**
	- *You taunt your Foe*
	- Effect: Target a Foe, that Foe must take its turn after this turn, if it hasn't taken it turn this round yet.
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]]
- **Full Throttle**
	- *You rev up your engine*
	- Effect: Gain 1d6 stacks of the [[Energized Condition]] per Round Number and then take a move action.
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]]
- **Leave me alone!**
	- *Description*
	- Effect: Target all nearby characters, force them to move 3d4 spaces away from you. Then take a Move Action.
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]] [[Push Trait]]
- **I won't give up!**
	- *You lose your patience and set your power loose*
	- Effect: Remove 2 stacks of the [[Impacted Condition]] if you have any.
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]]
- **Get in loser**
	- *You rush ahead, bringing along a friend*
	- Effect: Target an Ally adjacent to you, take a move action, the Ally maintains their relative position to you during this move action and any consecutive move actions.
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]] [[Move Trait|Move]]
- **Study Adversary**
	- *You observe your foe's movements*
	- Effect: Target a Unit, and ask the Game Master the max Health and Stamina each of its characters have.
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]]

Combo Actions:
- **Slide to a stop**
	- *You slide to a stop*
	- Condition: Your last action was a move action.
	- Effect: You may move an additional number of spaces equal to half the amount you moved with your last action, also gain stacks of [[Energized Condition|Energized]] equal to that amount. 
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]]
- **Isn't there a bounty on your head?**
	- *You threaten your foe*
	- Condition: Your last action targeted a Foe and did not have the [[Attack Trait]]
	- Effect: The foe gains the [[Hunted Condition]].
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
- **Now I'm motivated!**
	- *You are ready for the fight*
	- Condition: Your last action caused you to gain [[Empowered Condition]]
	- Effect: Roll your recovery die.
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]]
- **Sheathe Weapon**
	- *You put away your weapon*
	- Condition: You Exhausted a Foe's stamina or dealt damage to their health with your last action
	- Effect: The Foe gains the [[Impacted Condition]].
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]] [[Consumed Trait|Consumed]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
- **I won't lose!**
	- *You lose your patience and set your power loose*
	- Condition: Your current stamina is less than your Stamina pool
	- Effect: Roll your recovery die.
	- [[Stylish Trait|Stylish]]
