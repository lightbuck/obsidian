Remove your token from the board. You can't be targeted by any Character. 
This Blessing is removed at the end of your next Turn.
Your next action must either be a Combo Action calling out this Blessing in its Condition or a form of movement (Actions with the [[Move Trait]], [[Step Trait]] or [[Mobile Trait]]), which remove this Blessing.
When positioning yourself back on the Battlefield you must be within [[Ranges#Pathable Range 564a37|Pathable Range]] of your starting position, unless specified by an Effect of the Action you took.
