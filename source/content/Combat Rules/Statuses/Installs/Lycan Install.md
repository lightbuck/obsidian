Increase your size by one step, up to Huge. 
You can no longer make use of [[Area Armament|Area Armaments]], [[Ranged Armament|Ranged Armaments]] or actions from the [[Defensive Aspect]].

When you gain this condition, and at the beginning of every round, you gain a stack of [[Adrenaline]] if you Lose Access to at least 3 actions due to the aforementioned Armament restrictions. 

Any action you take gains the [[Hunting Trait]].
