The Forged Install can be one of three options
- Forged(Cymbal)
	-  Whenever you gain Stamina because of one of your actions, you gain an extra d6 for every AP spent on that action.
- Forged(Snare)
	-  You can take actions that require you to be Adjacent to a target from a space away.
- Forged(Tom)
	-  Whenever you inflict the [[Impacted Curse]] with a Melee Attack, you deal an extra 1d6 damage.
- Forged(Drum)
	-  When you target a foe with a Melee Attack you may target a second Foe adjacent to you or adjacent to the the original Target, deal half the damage you dealt to the original Foe to that second Foe.
