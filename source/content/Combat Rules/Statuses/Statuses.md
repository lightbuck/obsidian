Statuses are temporary effects.
# Cleansing
Removing a Status is referred to as Cleansing.
# Types of Statuses
## Hexes
Hexes are temporary negative effects which usually decay one stack at the time at the end of the affected character's turn. When you Cleanse a Hex you remove all of its stacks at once.
## Curses
Hexes are temporary negative effects. When you Cleanse a Curse you remove one of its stacks at a time.
## Blessings
Blessings are temporary beneficial effects which are usually removed upon the affected character doing something or upon something being done to the affected character. 
## Installs
An install is a type of status which changes your physical appearance and adds, removes or modifies parts of your character. When you are Knocked Out you remove the Install. Installs can't be cleansed.
