This module adds rules to create crew spaceships and to navigate Limbo and the Void.
# Ships
## Attributes
### Power
Each Room and Module Requires Power to function. Power can be redirected, deactivating Rooms or Components and activating others.
### Cargo Space
A ship's inventory size.
### Endurance
The overall health of the ship, when this number reaches 0 the GM determines what happens to the ship from this list:
- Detonation: The Ship explodes, dealing 1d4+1 damage to all Characters inside of it.
- Blackout: The Ship ceases to function and Characters count as if they are within Open Space even inside The Ship. The Ship is salvageable.
- Implosion: The Ship implodes, dealing 1d4+1 damage to all Characters inside of it. The Ship is salvageable.
- Sundering: The Ship is ripped apart and the Characters count as if they are within Open Space even inside The Ship. The Ship is salvageable.
### Speed
The speed of your ship
### Efficiency
The amount of Fuel saved each time you move. This value can be negative. 

## Components
### Rooms & Modules
Each ships has a different amount of slots in which its crew can choose to build a Room (a Crew operated facility used during downtime which uses 1 power) or a Module (an active ability for your Ship which use 2 power) 
Here is a sample list of some Rooms available for construction:
- Sleeping Quarters: dedicated room for R&R, enables Resting within the Ship.
- Kitchen: a dedicated room for creating nourishment, enables Resting within the Ship.
- Panorama: a room with monitors built into its walls to simulate any environment, enables Resting within the Ship.
- Med Bay: a single Character can rest here to recuperate all Health at once.
- Hold: +2 Cargo Space
- Hibernation Chamber: a chamber capable of containing a Character in suspending animation.
- Stellarium: a room which contains three dimensional projections of the stars used to plan routes. Whichever positive bonus you gain from Planning a Route is increased by 1.
- Library: a room containing books, tomes, data drives and a number of other devices for storing information. [[Skills#Intellect Skills|Intellect Skills]] gain +1 when rolled in this room.
Here are some Modules available for construction:
- Radar: Activate when Rolling for Danger to gain a -2 to the roll
- Emergency Jumper: Activate to leave the current Sector to an adjacent Sector chosen by the GM.
- Cloaking Device: Activate to hide your ship. The Ship is no longer hidden if you use another module or Weapon.
- Radiators: Deploy radiators to improve cooling for your Ship. Gain a temporary +1 Speed.
- Sealing Kit: A kit to repair damage to Your Ship. Activate to Heal 1 Endurance.
- Backup Generator: Use to quickly power something. Activate a Module a second time.
- Armament Rack: Activate to use an extra Armament that you chose when building this Module.
- Thaumatic Converter: A module capable of turning the Paracausal of magic into power for your ship. A character matching the [[Powers|Power]] chosen when Building this Module may activate this to gain +1 Power.
- Tractor Beam: Target one or more Characters to bring them aboard your Ship.

## Danger
When traversing space there may be hazards you could encounter, in certain dangerous situations the GM will ask the Crew to roll a 1d10.
- On a 4 or lower: There is nothing that may pose a threat to you.
- On a 5-8: There are hazards on the way but you noticed them first.
- On a 9 or higher: Something dangerous finds you before you notice it.

## Movement
### Travel
Ships have three types of movement, Subspace Travel, Space Travel, Atmospheric Travel.
#### Subspace Travel
To move between Realms, the ship must make use of Subspace Travel.
#### In-Realm Travel
The Normal movement of a ship.
#### Atmospheric Travel
Your ship may need to land on a planet this.
### Plotting a Route
When you plot a route you tell your GM where you want to head to and choose what kind of Route you are taking.
#### Shortcut Route
This Route grants +1 Speed or +1 Efficiency. Roll for Danger with a +1.
#### Careful Route
This Route Grants +1 Efficiency but -1 Speed.
#### Popular Route
This Route Grants +1 Speed but -1 Efficiency.
