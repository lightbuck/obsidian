## The Mandates from Heaven
Combat is **fast** and **stylish**
Build crafting is **flavorful** and **modestly deep**
Enemies should never interrupt Players
Players should interrupt enemies regularly
_Players can do only what they can describe._
### Inspirations
PF2e: The traits and action systems
Lancer: Smaller numbers, more health bars
Warframe: Complex before combat, simple in combat
Devil May Cry: Prioritizing fantasy over practicality

# Character Creation
#PH %%This needs a lot of work%%
There are two paths you can take to create your character:
Do you already have a character in mind? Then go through the rules as such
1. Choose one magic that best fits your character's identity, this is the **what** of your character
2. Choose the Styles that best fits your character's combat style, this is the **how** of your character
3. Choose an armor and make a weapon
4. Choose a Role that best fits your character's role in the team, this is the **why** of your character
5. Choose Skills that fit your character's backstory


This is the recommended path, as coming into the game with a strong idea for a character should help in chosing flavorful options

Do you not have a character idea in mind? Then go through the rules as such:
1. Browse availble Magic and choose one that seems interesting to you
2. Choose your two styles 
3. Choose an armor and make a wapon
4. Choose a role for your character, which should help you with determining their personality
5. Choose skills for your chaarcter, which should help you with determining their backstory
