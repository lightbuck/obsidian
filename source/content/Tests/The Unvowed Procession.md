# The Unvowed Cicada
Stamina: d20
Health: 10
Speed: 6
Size: Big
Action Points: 3
Turns: 2
Characters: 1
## Declaration
**Cinsidious Leeching**
*Tree sap drools from The Unvowed Cicada's mouth as it prepares to snatch prey.* 
The Unvowed Cicada will target any Character not belonging to [[The Unvowed Procession]] adjacent to it, choosing the one with the most [[Health and Stamina| Health]], dealing a point of damage to their [[Health and Stamina|Health]] and returning to its maximum [[Health and Stamina|Stamina]].
## Actions
Main Actions:
- **Claw strike**, 1 AP
	- *Description*
	- Effect: Target a Foe, deal 3d6 damage to them.
	- [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]]
- **Burrow**, 1 AP
	- *Description*
	- Effect: gain the [[Hidden Condition]]
	- -
- **Louder than a chainsaw**, 1 AP
	- *Description*
	- Effect: Target a Burst of size 5, deal 2d4 damage. Increase the stacks of any Character's [[Impacted Condition]] if they have any.
	- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Area Trait|Area]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]]
- **Ramming Bug**, 2 AP
	- *Description*
	- Effect: Take 2 Move actions. Target any Character you moved Adjacent to or you move through.  Deal 1d6 Damage.
	- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Move Trait|Move]] [[Fling Trait|Fling(5)]]

Combo Actions: 
- **Dragged Down**, 1 AP
	- *Description*
	- Condition: The Unvowed Cicada has damaged a Foe's Health with its last action.
	- Effect: Target the triggering Foe, they gain one stack of the [[Impacted Condition]] and the [[Hidden Condition]]. The Cicada gains the [[Hidden Condition]].
	- [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Afflict Trait|Afflict]] [[Melee Trait|Melee]] 
- **17 Year Strike**, 1 AP
	- *Description*
	- Condition: The Unvowed Cicada has the [[Hidden Condition]] 
	- Effect: Target a Foe, appear adjacent to them, deal 3d6 damage.
	- [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Attack Trait|Attack]] [[Surprising Trait|Surprising]]
- **Surprise Wing Strike**, 2 AP
	- *Description*
	- Condition: The Unvowed Cicada's last action was an Attack
	- Effect: Target two foes within 3 spaces of you, excluding the Target of you last Action, deal 2d6 damage.
	- [[Melee Trait|Melee]] [[Surprising Trait|Surprising]]
- **Larval Expulsion**, 1 AP
	- *Description*
	- Condition: The Unvowed Cicada has taken at least 9 damage to its health. 
	- Effect: The Unvowed Cicada removes all negative Conditions.
	- [[Escape Trait|Escape]]
