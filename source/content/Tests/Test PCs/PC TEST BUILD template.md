Abigail  Walker, alias 'The Burning Angel', is a young and proud vigilante which operates in the Western Realms of the Galactic Federations. She is a Sunnaut, but was born in the Federation. She wields The Phoenix Talon, and she has learned to fly with it so that she can move between the tall skyscrapers of the Federation with ease.
