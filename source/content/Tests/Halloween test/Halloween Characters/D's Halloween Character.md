## Identity
Super Hero Name: *Cavalry, Cavalry of House Nova*
Real Name: *Salvius*
Story: *Salvius is a Resono orphan, adopted by House Nova, a group of knights who trained him in their particular style of lance wielding. He makes up for his small statue by having a boisterous personality, but the only thing larger than his ego is his capacity for kindness.*
*Appearance: Salvius is 160 cm tall, he has pale skin, dark orange hair and fur and deep green eyes. He is covered head to toe in knightly plated armor, custom made to protect his ears and tail. The helmet has no lower part hiding the mouth, proudly displaying his everlasting smile. He wields a large spear with a thruster built into it.*
Combat Style: *Salvius surfs his spear into battle, using it to quickly move across the battlefield, he uses his momentum to deliver devastating blows. He also supports his team by using Resono Magic*
